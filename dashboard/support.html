Frequently asked questions

Helpful answers to common questions.

Check that you are using the correct account details and that your internet connection is working. If the problem continues, use the account recovery option if available or contact the official NEXTCHAIN support team.
If password management is enabled for your account, open Settings and look for the security options. If you have forgotten your password, use the official password recovery process. Never share your password or verification codes with another person.
Open the Deposit section and review the available payment methods and instructions. Only use payment details shown through an official, verified NEXTCHAIN channel. Deposit processing depends on the payment services configured for the platform.
Review the withdrawal status and any transaction reference available in your account. Processing times can vary by payment method. If the transaction remains unresolved, contact the official support team with the relevant reference. Do not send your password or private keys.
Visit the Investments section to review the information made available for each investment product. Carefully read the terms, fees, and risks before making a decision. Investment returns are not guaranteed unless explicitly supported by legally binding product terms.
Open the Transactions section to view the records available for your account. If a transaction does not appear, confirm its status with the relevant payment provider before attempting the payment again.
Some financial services require identity verification for security, fraud prevention, or regulatory compliance. If NEXTCHAIN requests verification, review the instructions through an official channel and understand how your information will be handled before submitting documents.
Use a unique password, secure your email account, and enable additional security features when available. Avoid suspicious links and never share passwords, one-time verification codes, wallet recovery phrases, or private keys. Support staff should not need your private key or recovery phrase.
Check the payment status with your payment provider and review any error message. Avoid repeating a payment until you have confirmed that the earlier attempt did not go through. Keep your transaction reference for any support request.
